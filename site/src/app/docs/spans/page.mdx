Every prompt becomes an OpenTelemetry-style tree of timed spans:

```text
session
└─ turn
   ├─ llm_call
   │  └─ thinking
   ├─ tool_call  (read · edit · bash · mcp)
   └─ subagent
      └─ … its own llm and tool spans
```

## Span fields

<Field name="span_type" type="enum" required>
  One of `llm`, `thinking`, `read`, `edit`, `bash`, `mcp`, `subagent`, `compaction`, `override`.
</Field>
<Field name="parent_span_id" type="ULID | null">
  The parent span. `null` for the root of a turn.
</Field>
<Field name="status" type="enum" required>
  `running`, `paused`, `ok`, `error`, `denied` or `edited`.
</Field>
<Field name="latency_ms" type="integer">
  Wall-clock duration in milliseconds.
</Field>
<Field name="cost_micro_usd" type="integer">
  Cost attributed to this node, in micro-dollars.
</Field>

## Waterfall view

The waterfall plots each span on a millisecond timeline, with parallel subagents in their own swim lanes. Clicking a bar jumps straight to its diff, thinking block or payload. Error spans get a rose badge; paused spans glow amber.

<Tip>Sort a session's spans by `cost_micro_usd` to find the one subagent that ate your budget.</Tip>
